## ✦ About

**For My Cutiepie** is a small interactive love letter built entirely for one special person.

It starts with a sealed envelope and slowly unfolds into a little world of **memories, music, photographs, reasons, and words that are easier to write in code than say out loud.**

> some things are better experienced than explained. ♡

---

## ✦ What's Inside

🌸 **Interactive Envelope** — the beginning of the surprise
🎵 **Background Music** — a soundtrack for the moment
📸 **Memory Gallery** — photos with captions and navigation
💌 **Reasons** — little things worth remembering
📝 **Personal Letter** — the part that matters most
✨ **Animations** — floating elements, petals, transitions & reveals
📱 **Responsive** — designed for both phones and desktops
